# Changelog

Firmware versions, newest first. The web page shows the notes for the latest one when your board is
behind. See "Versions" in the README for how to release one.

## 1.2.0

- Save up to 8 custom statuses. Each one joins the Status list and the swipe order under its own
  name, and can be deleted from the Status list.
- The page's live preview shows custom statuses saved from another computer too.

## 1.1.0

- Custom statuses sit exactly in the middle of the screen, even when the blank space can't split
  evenly between the LEDs: the picture moves half an LED instead.

## 1.0.0

- The clock carries on after a short unplug, a little behind, until the page syncs it again.
- Update the firmware from the web page.
- Two views: Original and Clock.
